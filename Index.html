Signal logic & limitations

Signals require at least 60 seconds of Coinbase observations, then compare 30- and 60-second returns. WAIT is the default. These signals are not trained AI, probabilities, or validated buy recommendations.

Never treat a price-direction signal as proof a Kalshi contract is profitable. Contract prices, fees, spreads, liquidity and settlement rules matter. Data may fail due to network restrictions or browser CORS. Keep this page open for updates; iOS may pause it in the background. No account credentials needed.
